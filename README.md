A make.fun entry for "One Line, Big Laugh" — one hilarious line, at most 200 characters, that lands entirely on its own. Creative angle: a smart fridge staging an intervention about midnight snacking.

## The entry

> My smart fridge staged an intervention: it locked the cheese drawer, dimmed its light like a disappointed parent, and whispered, 'It's 2 a.m., Dave.'

149 characters, 1 line. The fridge stops being an appliance and becomes the worried family member — locking the cheese drawer, dimming its light like a disappointed parent, and calling out the hour every midnight snacker recognises.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) verifies the entry against the gate in RULES.txt — UTF-8 text, 1 to 200 characters, not only white space, a single line — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
